**Phase A carries no third-party image binaries.** Every photographic/archival slot uses the
`ArtPlate` component — an art-directed CSS/SVG composition (warm duotone grounds + abstract
motifs) that stands in for a real photograph or archival plate. Each `ArtPlate` has a required
`aria-label` describing what it represents, so alt text is present sitewide. These placeholders
carry no licence obligations and cannot be mistaken for stock wellness imagery or for a real
person.

When real assets arrive, they are added here with full source · licence · attribution · alt, and
swapped in by changing the relevant content field. The **Library** records already carry genuine
public-domain source/rights information in `content/resources/library.ts` (Internet Archive
editions); their scanned plates can be dropped into the existing framed slots when sourced.
