build until the client supplies it — an empty footer slot is honest, a plausible-looking

## Organisational relationship — important, and new

> "We don't have exclusive Social for Yoga Mandala, as this is now an initiative under
> **Pranava Seva Trust**, you can add the socials for the same."

Yoga Mandala is **an initiative under Pranava Seva Trust**, not a standalone organisation.
It therefore has no social presence of its own and borrows the Trust's.

This has real design consequences and is not just a footer line:

- The site must not present Yoga Mandala as an independent institution with its own brand
  furniture. A parent-organisation credit belongs in the footer, and probably in About.
- It reinforces the client's existing position that Yoga Mandala represents no single school
  or lineage — it is a community initiative, hosted, not a competitor to its members.

### Instagram — two things to settle before this ships

1. **The share token is stripped, deliberately.** The client sent the URL with
   `?stkn=dXhhYWViZTZrM2dt` appended. That parameter is a personal share/attribution token
   generated by their own session; it does not belong in published markup and is dropped
   above. Link to the bare profile URL.
2. **The handle is personal, not organisational.** `thepranavshastri` reads as Praṇav's own
   account, but the request was for "the socials for Pranava Seva Trust". Ask whether the
   Trust or Praṇava Yogashāla has its own account that should be used instead, or whether
   the personal account is genuinely the intended one. Until answered, use it as given.

## OUTSTANDING — asked for, not yet supplied

- **Postal address.** It was requested in the same message and was not answered. Do not put
  a city, area or address anywhere on the site until the client provides one. "Bengaluru"
  appears in the *photo captions* as image provenance — that is a fact about where the
  pictures were taken, and is not a claim about a registered address.
- Public email address(es) — general and collaborations.
- A "submit an offering" destination.
- The production domain, needed for canonical URLs, sitemap and Open Graph tags.

## A fabricated claim found in the failed prototype — NEVER carry this forward

The archived build's `content/site.config.ts` contained:

```ts
register: { teachers: '700+ teachers across India and abroad' }
```

and rendered it on the homepage via the threshold section's `register` field.

**This number does not exist in any client source.** Verified by exhaustive search on
9 September 2026 across every file the client supplied:

- `Context/Website Pages Data.docx` — extracted and searched in full. The extraction is
  sound (it returns the known client sentences verbatim). No count of teachers, members or
  practitioners appears anywhere in it.
- `Context/Yoga-Mandala-V1-Project-Scope.md`, `Yoga-Mandala-Kickoff-Meeting-Prep.md`,
  `New Text Document.txt` — no match.
- `Pranava Website Blueprint.pdf` and `Yoga Mandala Portal Framework V1.pdf` — no match.

It was invented by the previous run and published as fact about the client's organisation.

**Rule going forward:** no number describing Yoga Mandala's size, reach, age or membership
appears anywhere on this site unless the client states it in writing and it is recorded on
this page with its source. This is the single most damaging category of error available to
us — a design flaw is embarrassing, a false claim about the client is a liability.
